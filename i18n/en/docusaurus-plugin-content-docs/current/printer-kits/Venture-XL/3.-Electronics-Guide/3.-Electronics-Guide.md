**Venture XL** uses a newly made control panel kit to give it life. It includes a 7-inch Pad based on CM68, a newly made Spider Pro, the SB\_Combo\_V2 that has been on the market for some time, and the latest version of the cartographer.  
They are connected together by CANBUS by default. The relationship is as follows:
