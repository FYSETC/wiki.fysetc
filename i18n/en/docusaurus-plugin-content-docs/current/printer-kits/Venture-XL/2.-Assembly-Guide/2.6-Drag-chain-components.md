# 2.6 Drag chain components

![](/images/venture-xl-assembly-guide-drag-chain-components--drag-chain-components.png)

## 2.6.1 X-Drag Chain
- Assemble according to the diagram；
- Open the movable buckle on the back of the drag chain and insert the CAN line and the light bar power line；
- Use M3x8 BHCS screws to fix one end of the drag chain to the extruder head and the other end to the profile；
- CAN line plugged into the CAN board；The back of the XY drag chain is fixed with a drag chain buckle

![](/images/venture-xl-assembly-guide-drag-chain-components--x-drag-chain.png)

### Gantry light bar power board
- The light bar cable is connected to port ①，See page 33；
- Port ② faces the drag chain and uses the power cable to connect to the CAN board；

  ![](/images/venture-xl-assembly-guide-drag-chain-components--gantry-light-bar-power-board.png)
  
## 2.6.2 Y-Drag Chain
- Open the movable buckle on the back of the drag chain and insert the CAN line power line；

![](/images/venture-xl-assembly-guide-drag-chain-components--y-drag-chain-01.png)

![](/images/venture-xl-assembly-guide-drag-chain-components--y-drag-chain-02.png)

## 2.6.3 Z-Drag Chain
- Attach the cooling fan to the tail of the motor；
- Place the fan wire and motor wire B into the aluminum profile slot and cover them with the aluminum profile slot cover.

  ![](/images/venture-xl-assembly-guide-drag-chain-components--z-drag-chain-02.png)
  
- Pass the AB motor wires and CAN board connection wires through the Z drag chain；
- Fix the drag chain with the drag chain fixings and tighten the screws；

  ![](/images/venture-xl-assembly-guide-drag-chain-components--z-drag-chain-01.png)


:::note

  **NOTE: Note where the cables pass through.**
