# 2.9 Rack and sealing acrylic

## 2.9.1 Material rack
- Assemble according to the diagram；
- The bracket is placed in the middle of the profile；

![](/images/venture-xl-assembly-guide-rack-and-sealing-acrylic--material-rack.png)

## 2.9.2 Sealing acrylic
### Material preparation
- Print the following prints, black PETG, 30% infill, 3 walls；
- Print link：https://github.com/FYSETC/Venture_XL/tree/main/STLs

![](/images/venture-xl-assembly-guide-rack-and-sealing-acrylic--material-preparation-01.png)

- Use a soldering iron to heat-weld the printed parts；

![](/images/venture-xl-assembly-guide-rack-and-sealing-acrylic--material-preparation-02.png)

### Camera assembly
- Assemble according to the diagram；
- Press the magnet into the print as shown；

![](/images/venture-xl-assembly-guide-rack-and-sealing-acrylic--camera-assembly-01.png)

- Open the camera box and take out the camera;
- Remove the bracket behind the camera from the middle connection;
- Install the camera body to the bracket and tighten it with screws；

![](/images/venture-xl-assembly-guide-rack-and-sealing-acrylic--camera-assembly-02.png)

- Install the door resistance assembly on the front assembly of the frame and tighten the screws；
- Route the camera power cable along the profile groove and install the profile groove cover，need to connect USB extension cable to the display interface（Because the camera cable is not long enough, you need to use a USB extension cable）.

![](/images/venture-xl-assembly-guide-rack-and-sealing-acrylic--camera-assembly-03.png)

### Door panel acrylic sheet installation
- Material preparation

![](/images/venture-xl-assembly-guide-rack-and-sealing-acrylic--door-panel-acrylic-sheet-installation-01.png)

- Insert “Door corner stopper” into the profile groove；

- Tighten it with screws；

![](/images/venture-xl-assembly-guide-rack-and-sealing-acrylic--door-panel-acrylic-sheet-installation-02.png)

- Insert “Door corner stopper” into the profile groove；

![](/images/venture-xl-assembly-guide-rack-and-sealing-acrylic--door-panel-acrylic-sheet-installation-03.png)

![](/images/venture-xl-assembly-guide-rack-and-sealing-acrylic--door-panel-acrylic-sheet-installation-05.png)

- Repeat the previous operation to assemble the remaining door；
- Installing the door on the profile frame；
- Tighten it with screws；

![](/images/venture-xl-assembly-guide-rack-and-sealing-acrylic--door-panel-acrylic-sheet-installation-06.png)
