##  X1/P1S Door Hinge Upgrade

![](/images/x1p1s-door-hinge--x1-p1s-door-hinge.png)


## Introduction：
The original hinge allows a maximum opening angle of 120 degrees. By replacing the original hinge, the door opening angle can be extended to 180 degrees or even 270 degrees, preventing users from accidentally damaging the glass door. The all-metal material is durable and reliable


## Applicable machine：
Bambu Lab P1S‌、Bambu Lab X1C/X1
