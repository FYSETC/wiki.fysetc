# Sparkx i7 LED Upgrade

## Product Introduction

![](/images/sparkx-i7-led-upgrade--113ed647f1d6fac6.png)

An upgrade based on the design of   [**“HLS - Hackman Led System for Sparkx i7”**](https://www.crealitycloud.com/model-detail/hackman-led-system-sparkx-i7?profileId=6a018bdd50932b397544644d&ts=1782201795026) with simplified installation and wiring.

## Compatible Printers：

:::warning

 Sparkx i7

:::

## Usage Steps
### Step 1
You need to download the STL file first for printing.

<img src="/images/sparkx-i7-led-system--step-1-01.png"
     width="600" />

<img src="/images/sparkx-i7-led-system--step-1-02.png"
     width="600" />

### Step 2
First, plug the terminal of the strip light's connecting wire into the control board.

<img src="/images/sparkx-i7-led-system--step-2.png"
     width="600" />

### Step 3
Assemble according to the diagram，Install the control board onto the shell printed in the first step.

<img src="/images/sparkx-i7-led-system--step-3.png"
     width="600" />

### Step 4
Install the printed part “8mm/5mm LED strip bracket.STL” into the slot below the X-axis and secure it using M3x6 screws.

<img src="/images/sparkx-i7-led-system--step-4.png"
     width="600" />

### Step 5
Install the printout “block-bottom-G.STL、block-top.stl” into the metal slot on the left side of the X-axis and M3x12mm secure it with screws.

<img src="/images/sparkx-i7-led-system--step-5.png"
     width="600" />

### Step 6
Secure the power cord with a cable tie.

<img src="/images/sparkx-i7-led-system--step-6.png"
     width="600" />

### Step 7
Plug the power module into the USB port of the machine.
After installation, touch the left side to switch the LED on or off.

<img src="/images/sparkx-i7-led-system--step-7.png"
     width="600" />
