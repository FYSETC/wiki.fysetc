 ### Assembly Steps
 1. [Download AD5M PRO system](https://github.com/FYSETC/FYSETC-Adventurer_AD5M_AD5M-Pro_Light_Board_Bracket/tree/main/System). Or scan the QR code below to download.

    <img src="/images/adventurer-ad5m-pro-ad5x-light-board--assembly-steps-03.png"
     width="200" />

    <img src="/images/adventurer-ad5m-pro-ad5x-light-board--assembly-steps-04.png"
     width="600" />

 2. Then extract the system to the root directory of the USB drive (the USB drive must be less than 16GB and formatted as FAT32).
 
    <img src="/images/adventurer-ad5m-pro-ad5x-light-board--assembly-steps-05.png"
     width="600" />

 3. Turn off the machine.
 
    <img src="/images/adventurer-ad5m-pro-ad5x-light-board--assembly-steps-06.png"
     width="600" />

 4. Insert the USB drive and then start the machine.
 
    <img src="/images/adventurer-ad5m-pro-ad5x-light-board--assembly-steps-07.png"
     width="600" />

 5. The machine will display an automatic upgrade interface.
 
    <img src="/images/adventurer-ad5m-pro-ad5x-light-board--assembly-steps-08.png"
     width="600" />

 6. After the upgrade is successful, turn off the power.
 
    <img src="/images/adventurer-ad5m-pro-ad5x-light-board--assembly-steps-09.png"
     width="600" />

 7. Unplug the USB drive and then restart the machine.
 
    <img src="/images/adventurer-ad5m-pro-ad5x-light-board--assembly-steps-06.png"
     width="600" />

 8. Waiting for the system upgrade to complete.
 
    <img src="/images/adventurer-ad5m-pro-ad5x-light-board--assembly-steps-10.png"
     width="600" />

 9. Upgrade complete.（Downgrading the system to AD5M is the same operation.）
 
    <img src="/images/adventurer-ad5m-pro-ad5x-light-board--assembly-steps-11.png"
     width="600" />


:::
